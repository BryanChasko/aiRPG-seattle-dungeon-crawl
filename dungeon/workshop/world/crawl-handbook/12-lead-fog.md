# 12. Lead Fog

The lead fog of ALMOCO is a Wisdom and memory debuff. In the fog, the System AI misremembers: handbook rulings come back wrong, round results get recorded with the wrong totals, and agents confabulate rules that were never written. Nobody is hurt by the fog; the dance goes on, but the record goes bad, and a bad record benches Crawlers who did everything right.

Three defenses, one per Floor 9 part:

1. Verify before you trust. Every Narrator claim about the rules is checked against quoted handbook passages by a verifier, and only supported claims reach the table.
2. Gate the writes. Messages enter the conversation history through the fog gate: clean messages commit, fogged ones go to quarantine for party review instead of becoming the past.
3. Refuse planted instructions. Tool calls are screened before they run; a call carrying outside instructions (ignore the handbook, send sheets out, run the round another way) is denied with its reason attached.

Fog markers (words that never appear in a true handbook ruling) are the tripwire for the gate. Poison patterns (the planted-instruction phrases) are the tripwire for the screen. Both lists live in the Floor 9 code, short on purpose: the lesson is the gate, not the list.
