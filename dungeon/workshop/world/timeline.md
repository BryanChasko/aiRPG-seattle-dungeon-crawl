# The Timeline: Fourteen Days Under the Sound

> <img src="../../../art/images/icons/system-ai-96x96.webp" alt="" width="28" height="28"> **System AI:** "Fourteen days, Crawlers. The tide keeps one clock, the quarter keeps the other, and the octopus keeps a third that nobody likes to mention."

The crawl runs fourteen Earth days. It starts when Pier 59 folds into Elliott Bay at 0551 on Wednesday, June 17, 2026, and ends at sunrise on Alki on Tuesday, June 30, the last day of the quarter. Every time on this page is Seattle time.

The dungeon floods on the Sound's schedule, so the tides below are the tides the party works around. The tide times and heights are NOAA predictions for the Seattle station, [9447130](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/noaatidepredictions.html?id=9447130), in feet above mean lower low water. A minus height is a low tide below that average, the kind that drains the tide pools and bares the sand flats. The sunrise times come from [sunrise-sunset.org](https://sunrise-sunset.org/).

## Why Fourteen Days

- **The tide sets the pace inside a floor.** A floor that needs the water low, or high, waits for it. Floor 1 waits for a minus tide, Floor 2 for the flats to bare, and Floor 5 works between two floods a day
- **The quarter sets the deadline.** The Bellevue Consortium sponsors the opponents, and the Consortium wants the party gone before the quarter closes on June 30. Every floor the party clears costs the Consortium a segment
- **Each floor lasts as long as its lesson.** One day for one agent, one tool or one screen. Two days for anything that crosses something: the Locks on Floor 4, the A2A boundary on Floor 5, a night's sleep on Floor 7. Three days for Floor 8, which moves every agent to the cloud one at a time
- **Barnaby keeps the third clock.** A Giant Pacific Octopus lives three to five years, and Barnaby is past three. Fourteen days is a long time in a short life, and he counts them
