# Titles, Skills and Fusion

Titles are passive bonuses the System awards for remarkable or ridiculous accomplishments. A Crawler may hold several; the System announces each one.

- Salish Sea Guardian: +15% movement on wet or slippery terrain; immune to water-based environmental debuffs. Cordula's starting title.
- First Final Beat: +1 to your next three Final Beat rolls.
- Ferry Line Survivor: patience; advantage on Endurance calls when you are outnumbered.

Skills are unlocked by achievements, skill books from loot boxes, trainers in Safe Rooms, or practice: use a skill ten times in dance-offs and it gains a level.

Fusion: two Level 3 skills held by members of the same party can be fused at a Safe Room forge into one new skill. Example: Ink Cloud (Barnaby) and Saltwater Jet (Shelly) fuse into Inkjet, a long-range blinding spray.

Exploits: the System respects a combination it did not see coming, and pays out a loot box for it the first time.
