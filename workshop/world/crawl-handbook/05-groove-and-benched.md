# Groove, Mana, Benched and Cancelled

Groove is how long a Crawler can stay on the dance floor. Mana is how many abilities a Crawler can use before resting. Groove and Mana are derived stats: the Character Agent computes them when a Crawler is created, and nobody chooses either value.

- Groove = 10 + twice the Constitution modifier + level, never less than 4.
- Mana = 5 + the Intelligence modifier + the Wisdom modifier, never less than 1.

A Crawler regains all Groove and Mana by resting in a Safe Room.

Benched: at 0 Groove, a Crawler leaves the dance floor. The Crawler wakes up in the last Safe Room visited with 1 Groove, loses the loot box from that dance-off, and loses half of the Crawler's Hype, rounded down. Nobody dies in the crawl.

Cancelled: a Crawler benched three times on the same floor has the segment cancelled for the rest of the floor. The Crawler rides the elevator down to the next floor with no loot from the floor the Crawler left.

Companions such as Shelly and Chonks have their own Groove. A benched companion returns to the companion's egg and hatches again at the next Safe Room.
