# Species

The System offers a species at registration. Species is not heritage or ancestry: species is the kind of creature the dungeon decided a character is this morning. Each species grants one trait.

| Species | Trait |
|---|---|
| Human (Seattle Native) | Rain-Proof: immune to Soggy; advantage on checks to navigate the city above |
| Human (Transplant) | Fresh Eyes: once per floor, reroll a failed Read the Room check |
| Uplifted Giant Pacific Octopus | Eight Arms: may assist and dance in the same round; needs salt water within reach |
| Uplifted Crow | Remembers Faces: advantage against any opponent that character has danced with before |
| Harbor Seal | Slick: +2 Footwork on wet floors |
| Sea Otter | Tool User: carries one extra item; +1 Choreography |
| Banana Slug | Unbothered: immune to Off-Beat from heckling; moves slowly |
| Fremont Troll-kin | Under the Bridge: +2 Presence in any dance-off held under a bridge or an overpass |
| Underground Ghost | Pioneer Square Regular: passes through one locked door per floor |

Pronouns are recorded on every character sheet, and the System AI uses each character's pronouns. Ask; never assume.
