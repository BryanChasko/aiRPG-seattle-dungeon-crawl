# World Guide

> <img src="../../images/icons/system-ai-96x96.webp" alt="" width="28" height="28"> **System AI:** "Read this before you build me. I would rather you knew what you were building."

Everything the agents say about the world comes from these pages. The prompts, the Rules Agent's knowledge base and the art are built from these pages.

- [The Story](story.md): the adventure, told in Cordula's log, floor by floor, with Barnaby waking up one Strands concept at a time.
- [The Timeline](timeline.md): the fourteen Earth days of the crawl, the tides on each day, and how old everyone is on the morning the pier folds.
- [The Cast](cast.md): character sheets for the party, in the order they join, and for the System AI that runs the crawl. Childhoods, likes and dislikes, stats, gear, abilities and achievements.
- [The System](the-system.md): the LitRPG design guide. Origin, interface, stats, sub-genre, builds, economy, conflict and consequence, mapped to the workshop floor that builds each layer.
- [Seattle, Under the Sound](seattle.md): whose land this is, the eight dungeon floors and their Puget Sound habitats, the cities, the opponents and local culture.
- [The Crawl Handbook](crawl-handbook/): the rules. Ability scores, checks, dance-offs, the Final Beat, Groove and being benched, stealth, Hype and loot, species, classes, titles, Safe Rooms. The Rules Agent searches these files on Floor 5, and a Bedrock knowledge base indexes these files on Floor 8.

The crawl is nonlethal by design: conflict is a dance-off or a clever way around a dance-off, and a Crawler who loses is benched, never killed.
